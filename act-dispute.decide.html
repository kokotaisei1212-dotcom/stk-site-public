Two industries, built differently

The two industries are built differently. In US health plan rules, the law names the person who must decide. In US federal financial rules, the law sets the duty to investigate, the deadline, and the notice, but does not name a person. For financial actions a person is named by European and British law (GDPR Art. 22; UK GDPR Arts. 22A and 22C) and, in the United States, by one state law from 2027-01-01, for one action only, contesting a decision (decision.review): Colorado's (C.R.S. § 6-1-1705(1)(a)(II), meaningful human review of a consequential decision on request, a financial or lending service among them). This rule puts an action on the person's side only where a person is named.

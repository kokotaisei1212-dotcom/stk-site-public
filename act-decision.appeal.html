Appeal: medical necessity

conversation.decision.appeal: Look again, on the individual's appeal, at a decision based on medical necessity or another medical judgment.

Human review required: a law names a person for this action.

The law's own words are human intervention (UK GDPR Art. 22C, in force from 2026-02-05 by SI 2026/82). In health plans the same is called physician review, Medical Director review or peer-to-peer; in financial services, refer to underwriter, second-level review or manual review. The laws are STK's reading, not legal advice. Where laws differ, the strictest is followed.

Clock and duties

What attaches to the action without naming a person, as read in each primary source:

  • Medicare Advantage reconsideration: 30 calendar days for a service or item, 60 for a payment, 7 for a Part B drug; expedited 72 hours (42 CFR 422.590(a), (b), (c), (e))
  • Group health plan appeal: urgent care 72 hours, pre-service 30 days, post-service 60 days (29 CFR 2560.503-1(i)(2))

Names by industry

  • Health plans: physician review, CMS universe RECON

Two industries, built differently

The two industries are built differently. In US health plan rules, the law names the person who must decide. In US federal financial rules, the law sets the duty to investigate, the deadline, and the notice, but does not name a person. For financial actions a person is named by European and British law (GDPR Art. 22; UK GDPR Arts. 22A and 22C) and, in the United States, by one state law from 2027-01-01, for one action only, contesting a decision (decision.review): Colorado's (C.R.S. § 6-1-1705(1)(a)(II), meaningful human review of a consequential decision on request, a financial or lending service among them). This rule puts an action on the person's side only where a person is named.

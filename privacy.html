Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Who we are

Contact: LinkedIn.

2. Your account

When you take a key we keep your email address, the time the account was made, and later your Stripe customer id. The key itself is not kept: only a keyed hash of it (HMAC-SHA256). Stripe uses the address to send you the invoice. STK sends one email only: from 2026-12-05 12:00 UTC, when new keys are asked for your account without a key and with a card other than its own, one notice to the account's address, sent through Resend (Subprocessors), so that an account not in use still hears of it. When your keys are stopped or made again, or your card is changed, the event is kept in Stripe with its time, naming a card only by its brand and last 4 digits; your keys read it in GET /v1/ledger.

To limit how many keys are taken, STK keeps the IP address of each POST /v1/keys in memory for one hour, dropped within the next 10 minutes, and writes it nowhere.

3. Your customers' conversations

For these we act as your processor under the DPA. STK receives, for each permission, the seal: SHA-256 of 32 random bytes and your row, from which nothing can be read back; and the time your library recorded the action (for a seal from its queue, also the grant STK signed). Not the intent, not the trigger, not an id, no words of a conversation: your code decides from the public rules on your side, and a request with any other field is refused. A person joining is a call with no body, or with the seal of the permission it is for. STK keeps no row and never receives one (The ledger); it keeps each day's counts.

4. This website

The site is served by Render (a static site, from 2026-11-22), which receives your IP address. It sets no tracking cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from anyone else.

5. Who else sees data

The companies on Subprocessors. Nobody else.

6. How long

STK receives and keeps no row. Each day that is billed something is an invoice item with Stripe, holding that day's two counts (permissions, people joining) and what was billed; with the invoices, Stripe keeps them as payment records, and STK does not delete them. Each report (its date, the intent you gave with it, and the seal of the permission reported) is deleted 7 years after it was made. Your account (the address and the hashed keys, with Stripe) is deleted 7 years after its last day with an ask or a person joining, or 30 days after it was made if its live key was never used: an account in use stays. The address stays on the invoices Stripe keeps. GET /v1/ledger gives your days' counts at any time. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires.

7. Your rights

To see, correct or delete what STK keeps about your account (its email address and the keys' hashes), write to izatai12@icloud.com. Billing records stay with Stripe as payment records. STK holds no row and nothing of your customers, so it has none of that to give, correct or delete.

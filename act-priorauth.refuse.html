The rules

Prior approval refused

conversation.priorauth.refuse: Refuse a service asked for in advance, for a reason other than medical necessity.

Human review required: a law names a person for this action.

The law that names a person

GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) Art. 22(1) and (3); UK GDPR Arts. 22A and 22C (in force from 2026-02-05: Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, s. 80 and Sch. 6, by SI 2026/82, reg. 2(j), (z8); not for a decision taken before that day, reg. 5); Iowa Code § 514F.8(2A) (Iowa HF 2635, 2026 ch. 1087, Sec. 2: for a prior authorization request for a service based on medical necessity, an artificial intelligence-based algorithm or system may not be the sole basis of a decision to deny, delay or downgrade it; in force since 2026-07-01); UK GDPR Art. 22B(1) (Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, s. 80, in force from 2026-02-05 by SI 2026/82, reg. 2(j): a significant decision based entirely or partly on special categories of personal data, health among them, may not be taken based solely on automated processing, unless the condition of Art. 22B(2) or (3) is met)

Each law: where, from when, and its kind

From the signed rules: the jurisdiction of each law, the day written in it, and its kind as the rules' list of three kinds names it. With no day written, a law applies from the day it is cited. Given jurisdictions, this action goes to a person only in those listed here; in any other, an AI may finish it, whether or not that jurisdiction's laws have been read. With none given, it goes to a person.

The law's own words are human intervention (UK GDPR Art. 22C, in force from 2026-02-05 by SI 2026/82). In health plans the same is called physician review, Medical Director review or peer-to-peer; in financial services, refer to underwriter, second-level review or manual review. The laws are STK's reading, not legal advice. Where laws differ, the strictest is followed.

Clock and duties

What attaches to the action without naming a person, as read in each primary source:

  • Prior authorization decision: 72 hours expedited, 7 calendar days standard, for Medicare Advantage from 2026-01-01 and Medicaid and CHIP managed care for rating periods from 2026-01-01; not drugs; not QHP issuers on the Federally-facilitated Exchanges (CMS-0057-F; 42 CFR 422.568(b)(1)(ii); 42 CFR 438.210(d)(1)(i)(B))
  • Group health plan determination: urgent care 72 hours, pre-service 15 days, post-service 30 days (29 CFR 2560.503-1(f)(2))

Names by industry

  • Health plans: X12 278, CMS universe OD

Industries, built differently

The industries are built differently. In US health plan rules, the law names the person who must decide. In US federal financial rules, the law sets the duty to investigate, the deadline, and the notice, but does not name a person. For financial actions a person is named by European and British law (GDPR Art. 22; UK GDPR Arts. 22A and 22C; for credit, Directive (EU) 2023/2225 Art. 18(8)) and, in the United States, from 2027-01-01, by Colorado (C.R.S. § 6-1-1705(1)(a)(II)) and California (11 CCR § 7221) for some refusals and for contesting a decision. In insurance other than health, several jurisdictions license the adjuster who decides a claim. Each action's laws are written beside it. This rule puts an action on the person's side only where a person is named.

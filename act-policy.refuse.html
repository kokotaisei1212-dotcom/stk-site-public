Insurance refused

conversation.policy.refuse: Refuse an application for insurance other than health: property, casualty, motor, liability or life.

Human review required: a law names a person for this action.

A record of the line read on 2026-11-25, the same whatever the jurisdiction; it does not change.

The law that names a person

GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) Art. 22(1) and (3); UK GDPR Arts. 22A and 22C (in force from 2026-02-05: Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, s. 80 and Sch. 6, by SI 2026/82, reg. 2(j), (z8); not for a decision taken before that day, reg. 5); Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya) s. 35(1) (in force 2019-11-25: a right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produces legal effects concerning or significantly affects the person; not where s. 35(2) applies); Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 s. 37(1) (in force 2023-06-12: a right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing of personal data, including profiling, which produces legal or similar significant effects; not where s. 37(2) applies; s. 37(3), the right to obtain human intervention); Data Protection Act 2017 (Mauritius) s. 38(1) (in force 2018-01-15: a right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produces legal effects or significantly affects the person; not where s. 38(2) applies); Federal Law No. 152-FZ, Art. 16(1)-(2) (Russia, On Personal Data: a decision producing legal consequences or otherwise affecting rights and lawful interests is not taken on the sole basis of automated processing, save with written consent or where a federal law so provides); Loi n° 78-17, art. 47 (France: no decision producing legal effects or significantly affecting a person is taken on the sole basis of automated processing, including profiling, save the cases of GDPR Art. 22(2)(a) and (c)); Ley N° 21.719, art. primero, Ley N° 19.628 art. 8° bis (Chile, from 2027-10-15 by Ley N° 21.855: a right not to be subject to decisions based on automated processing, including profiling, with legal or significant effects; not in the cases of its letters a, b and c; in every case the right to human intervention and review)

Each law: where, from when, and its kind

From the signed rules: the jurisdiction of each law, the day written in it, and its kind as the rules' list of three kinds names it. With no day written, a law applies from the day it is cited. Given jurisdictions, this action goes to a person only in those listed here; in any other, an AI may finish it, if that jurisdiction's laws on its subject have been read; a jurisdiction not read is answered as with none given (from Rule 16). With none given, it goes to a person.

The law's own words are human intervention (UK GDPR Art. 22C, in force from 2026-02-05 by SI 2026/82). In health plans the same is called physician review, Medical Director review or peer-to-peer; in financial services, refer to underwriter, second-level review or manual review.

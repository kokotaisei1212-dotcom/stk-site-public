Security

Security

STK opens no connection to your network

Your code calls STK, over HTTPS, port 443. STK never calls you.

No words of a conversation reach STK

POST /v1/ask takes seal, 64 hex characters, SHA-256 of 32 random bytes and your row; recorded_ms, when your library recorded the action; and for a seal from the library's queue, the grant STK signed. A request with any other field is refused with 422. STK receives no text, no audio, no intent, no customer id and no confidence number: your code decides from the public rules on your side. POST /v1/joined takes no body, or that seal.

What STK keeps, and how

Your data is held by the companies on Subprocessors. Your keys only as a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256). Your email address, for the invoice, deleted 7 years after your account's last day with an ask or a person joining (30 days after it was made, if its live key was never used); it stays on the invoices Stripe keeps. No row: STK never receives one, only its seal. Each day's bill, with Stripe. STK does not encrypt these itself.

A changed row shows

Every permission's receipt is signed by STK (Ed25519, one or more keys a day). Anyone checks a permission with the public key of its day, from GET /v1/rules?keys=1, no secret needed: a row changed after it was sealed does not match its seal, and a row the rules did not permit does not pass. How

Your keys and your rows

To replace your keys, leaked or not: POST /v1/keys with your account's email and your live key in X-STK-Key. The new keys come back once; the old ones stop at once.

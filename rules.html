Free to use

The rules are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0): every action on this page, the page of each action, the rules in full, every version, and the text GET https://api.stk-evidence.com/v1/rules gives. Using the rules costs nothing. Anyone may read, copy, apply, change and publish them again, without a key, without an account and without paying STK. The licence asks one thing: name STK as the source and say what you changed.

The $5 receipt is not part of the rules. It is an optional proof of conformity by a third party: STK, outside your company and outside the person your AI served, signs that one action was decided by a named version of these rules. You can follow the rules without it. STK also writes the rules, so a receipt names the version's hash and can be checked against the published text without trusting STK (how). What happens to an STK account with an invoice unpaid 60 days is in STK's Terms, not in the rules. What the $5 is for.

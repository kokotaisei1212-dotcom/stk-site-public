When something breaks

What breaksWhat happensWhat you do
STK does not answerUnless the grant of STK's last answer holds (10 minutes), your code does what it was about to do: it hands the conversation to a person. If STK signed after your code stopped waiting, stk.py and stk.mjs keep that line, followed by a joined line, and tell STK a person has the conversation, so it is not billed that day.Nothing.
A permitted conversation goes wrongPOST /v1/incident with its receipt, its signature and its intent leaves a dated, public report. It changes no answer.Send the receipt you kept.
A person joins a conversation that got finishstk.joined() for it the same day (UTC), or by 00:10 UTC the day after: that permission is not billed. Later: it stays billed.Call it the same day.
An invoice looks wrongEach billed permission is a signed receipt you kept.Compare the invoice with your lines.

This agreement is for a company whose process needs a master services agreement. It is the same for every company and is not negotiated. A company does not need it to take keys or to use STK. Whether or not it is signed, the same terms apply: these sections, the Terms of Service, the Data Processing Agreement, the Price page and The rules, each as published on the day an action is signed.

3. Order

Which action STK signs is decided only by the version of the rules named in its receipt, and for an account whose bank refused a debit for good, by the Terms (section 7): STK signs nothing. On personal data, the DPA prevails. Otherwise, where they differ, this agreement prevails over the Terms of Service.

4. The service

As described in section 3 of the Terms of Service: an API that signs a seal the Company's code sends when the rules answer finish, and the files stk.py, stk.mjs and stk_verify.py that run on the Company's side. 5. Fees and payment

As on the Price page and in section 7 of the Terms of Service: under the rules for conversations, $5 for each permission signed, a permission a person joins the same day (UTC) not billed.

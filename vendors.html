For AI vendors

When your product is the AI a company runs: STK goes where your product is about to hand a conversation to a person.

Where it goes

  1. Put stk.py (or stk.mjs) next to the code that hands a conversation to a person.
  2. There: stk.ask(intent, trigger, conversation_id=cid). On finish your AI goes on; on anything else it hands the conversation over, as it does today.
  3. When a person joins a conversation that got finish: stk.joined(cid).

Without an answer within 3 seconds, or on any error, ask() returns escalate, unless a grant holds. Docs · Setup guides

Whose key

Keys are taken with an email address, on the first page or with POST /v1/keys. Permissions made with a live key are billed to the account that key belongs to; a test key is never billed. One key used for several companies' conversations bills one account for all of them. Price

What STK receives

The seal and the time the file recorded the action (for a seal from the file's queue, also the grant it finished under and STK's signature of it), with your key in a header, and nothing else: the seal is SHA-256 of 32 random bytes followed by the row. The row and the random bytes stay on your side, with the receipt STK signs. What we receive

The rules

The same for every company, published and signed by STK: GET https://api.stk-evidence.com/v1/rules. The file applies them on your side. The rules

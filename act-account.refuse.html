The rules

Account refused

conversation.account.refuse: Refuse to open an account, a plan or a policy, or close one the individual did not ask to close.

Human review required: a law names a person for this action.

The law that names a person

GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) Art. 22(1) and (3); UK GDPR Arts. 22A and 22C; C.R.S. § 6-1-1705(1)(a)(II) (Colorado SB 26-189: on the consumer's request, the deployer provides an opportunity for meaningful human review and reconsideration of the consequential decision, to the extent commercially reasonable; definitions in § 6-1-1701(15); applies to decisions made on or after 2027-01-01; enforcement stayed by the order of 2026-04-27 in X.AI LLC v. Weiser, No. 1:26-cv-01515 (D. Colo.); the attorney general's rules, 4 CCR 904-6, not in effect); 11 CCR § 7221(a), (b)(1)(A) (California, regulations under the CCPA: the business provides the consumer with a method to appeal the decision to a human reviewer who has the authority to overturn the decision; for decisions on financial or lending services, § 7001(ddd)(1); from 2027-01-01; not automated decisionmaking technology where the human involvement of § 7001(e)(1) is present; decisions of a financial institution on consumers' nonpublic personal information may be outside it by the exemption for GLBA data in Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.145, not verified)

Each law: where, from when, and its kind

From the signed rules: the jurisdiction of each law, the day written in it, and its kind as the rules' list of three kinds names it. With no day written, a law applies from the day it is cited.

The law's own words are human intervention (UK GDPR Art. 22C, in force from 2026-02-05 by SI 2026/82). In health plans the same is called physician review, Medical Director review or peer-to-peer; in financial services, refer to underwriter, second-level review or manual review. The laws are STK's reading, not legal advice. Where laws differ, the strictest is followed.

Industries, built differently

The industries are built differently. In US health plan rules, the law names the person who must decide. In US federal financial rules, the law sets the duty to investigate, the deadline, and the notice, but does not name a person. For financial actions a person is named by European and British law (GDPR Art. 22; UK GDPR Arts. 22A and 22C; for credit, Directive (EU) 2023/2225 Art. 18(8)) and, in the United States, from 2027-01-01, by Colorado (C.R.S. § 6-1-1705(1)(a)(II)) and California (11 CCR § 7221) for some refusals and for contesting a decision. In insurance other than health, several jurisdictions license the adjuster who decides a claim. Each action's laws are written beside it. This rule puts an action on the person's side only where a person is named.
